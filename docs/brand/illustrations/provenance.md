References to “image 1,” “image 2,” and the “supplied concept” describe those generation inputs. The curated examples and [current guide](../illustrations.md) provide the references for future work.

Generated screenshots may alter UI details despite instructions to preserve them. Their logos, typography, controls, sample data, and movie posters must not become replacement app assets. Pixel coordinates and size requests in the historical prompts express editing intent and do not establish CSS dimensions or responsive behavior. Only the illustration direction and the selected composition are reference material.
