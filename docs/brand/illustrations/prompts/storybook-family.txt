Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: A single landscape art-direction concept board for Fantasy Reel, a movie fantasy league app. This is an illustration-style exploration, not production assets and not a screenshot.
Create one beautifully composed, polished landscape presentation with three distinct editorial spot-illustration vignettes in one cohesive visual language, equal spaced columns on a shared near-black #0f0f0f background, generous margins. Quiet title top left reading "A night at Fantasy Reel" in chunky playful sophisticated sans-serif lettering inspired by the character of Bricolage Grotesque. A small subtitle reads "Illustration direction".
Three labels beneath the vignettes, simple soft-white sans-serif, exactly: "Your leagues", "Trading block", "Wishlist".
Left vignette (Your leagues): A charming miniature independent neighborhood cinema entrance, its open double doors casting a warm matte paper-gold pool of light, a few burgundy cinema seats and a curling film strip. Emphasize a welcoming evening with friends and anticipation, not an abandoned building. No extra lettering on the cinema. An abstract blank poster outside.
Center vignette (Trading block): Two graceful human hands from opposite sides exchanging two small illustrated movie-poster cards, one with a gold moon landscape and one with a burgundy sun, with one curled perforated film ribbon loosely linking the composition. Cropped expressive hands only, no full characters or faces. Make the offered exchange very clear. Each card is visually distinct, handmade and tactile.
Right vignette (Wishlist): A carefully fanned stack of three small stylized movie-poster cards with original abstract landscape art, an oversized burgundy heart-shaped bookmark tucked over the front card, a little curled paper corner. Hopeful, collected favorites.
Style across all three: Sophisticated 2D editorial illustration, playful slightly irregular cut-paper silhouettes, confident simplified rounded shapes, mild exaggerated proportions, limited two-plane depth, subtle screen-printed grain confined to shapes. Think tactile independent film-festival programme art. Charming and grown-up. Strong silhouettes that read at small in-app sizes. Moderate detail, about 3-5 main shapes per scene with necessary supporting details. No enclosing white rectangles behind scenes. No glossy rendering, bevels, metallic gold, photorealism, heavy 3D shadows, neon, stock corporate characters, emoji appearance, confetti, sparkles, Oscars statuettes, existing movie IP, or imitation of any named artist. Do not recreate or redesign the app's logo.
Palette: dominant charcoal #1c1c1c, matte mustard gold #c9a227, warm paper #ead8b3, dusty burgundy #a8505c, soft white #e8e8e8; very sparing muted blue-gray #718691 accents on poster landscapes only. The last two art colors warm paper and blue-gray are proposed illustration accents, not interface changes. Texture and warm colors should make the dark app inviting. Ensure the artwork's charcoal forms are distinguishable against near-black via warm edges and paper facets. Keep text outside the artwork. Art direction should feel coherent with a gold film-strip speech-bubble brand and warm conversational movie fandom. Wide format, refined spacing, large artwork, no captions beyond the specified heading, subtitle, and three labels.
