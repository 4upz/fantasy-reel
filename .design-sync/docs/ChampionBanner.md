# ChampionBanner

The result of a completed season, on that league's dashboard and standings
page. It is the one gold-lit surface in the app: `.champion-plate` is the only
gradient allowed on any page, and only one instance of it may appear.

Co-champions are a first-class case, not an edge case. Two teams tied at rank
one both hold the title, so the eyebrow reads "Co-champions" and a caption
spells the tie out — without those words, two gold rank chips in the standings
below read as a rendering bug.
