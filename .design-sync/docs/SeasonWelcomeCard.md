# SeasonWelcomeCard

The first thing a member sees in a season they were carried into by their
commissioner.

It exists to defuse the single biggest confusion in the seasons feature: you
follow a notification into a league with no movies and read "where did my
roster go?" The card says the roster is *supposed* to be empty before that can
be misread, and links back to the standings that closed the last season.
Dismissal is remembered per league.
