# SeasonHistoryList

Every season of one league, newest first, on `/league/[id]/history`.

Years run down a hairline spine on the left — the film-can label repeating down
the page. That is the only ornament, because the list grows by one row a year
forever and has to stay readable at fifteen rows, not just three. Runners-up
carry their real rank, so a shared first place correctly shows a 3rd with no
2nd.
